Auto-create Groups
Re-creates every slot of the chosen group that is not pinned (📌) with 3 DPS + 1 Support per party. Pin the characters you want to keep on the raid pages first. Each click gives a slightly different variation, and Undo brings the previous state back. Serca and Cathedral can be switched between Party 1 and Party 2; auto-create uses only characters assigned to the selected group. A class never appears twice in the same party. The minimum ilvl follows each raid's difficulty (NM / HM / NiM). NiM has priority over HM, HM over NM. If a group cannot be completed, it still gets every eligible character that is left. With Balance combat power on, characters placed by this run are swapped between the parties of the same difficulty (NiM, HM and NM each on their own) until their combat power is as even as possible; priority and all other rules stay untouched.
